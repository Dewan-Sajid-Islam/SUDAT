Stage 5 — Energy Budget Diagnosis

Purpose:
Determine whether the expansion-history discrepancy is caused by premature scalar-field domination.

Results:
- Matter dominates (~96.9%) at z = 8.
- Scalar field dominates (~99.9%) today.
- Smooth transition between the two sectors.
- H varies only from ~14.08 to ~15.07 across z = 8 → 0.

Conclusion:
The poor agreement with ΛCDM is not caused by incorrect matter/scalar fractions.
The discrepancy likely originates from the current background evolution equations rather than the energy-budget transition itself.
