Questions should only be removed once they have been answered through mathematical derivation, numerical validation, or observational testing.

# Research Principle

No equation should be modified solely to improve observational agreement.

Every future modification must arise from a physically motivated theoretical argument and must be tested against the complete Version 1 validation suite.
