# SUDAT Research Program — Design Principles

This document defines the guiding principles for all future developments of SUDAT.

These principles are intended to ensure that future generations of the theory remain scientifically disciplined and physically motivated.

---

# Principle 1 — Preserve Validated Physics

No future modification should discard an already validated mechanism unless there is strong mathematical or observational evidence that it is incorrect.

Validated mechanisms include:

* Density-induced symmetry breaking
* Critical density
* Stable vacuum
* Matter-like oscillatory branch
* Dark-energy branch
* Positive-energy solutions

---

# Principle 2 — Physics Before Fitting

No equation may be modified solely to improve agreement with observations.

Every modification must arise from a physically motivated argument.

Observational improvement should be a consequence of the theory, not the motivation for constructing it.

---

# Principle 3 — Minimal Assumptions

Whenever possible, reduce rather than increase the number of independent assumptions.

A derivation is preferred over a postulate.

A postulate is preferred over an arbitrary parameter.

---

# Principle 4 — Version Control

Effective Theory v1.0 remains permanently frozen.

Every future generation must be evaluated against Version 1.

If a new generation fails to outperform Version 1 scientifically, Version 1 remains the reference implementation.

---

# Principle 5 — Complete Validation

Every future theory must successfully pass the full validation suite:

* Vacuum stability
* Symmetry breaking
* Healthy parameter region
* Background evolution
* Energy budget
* Field dynamics
* Expansion history

Future observational tests will include:

* Structure formation
* Matter power spectrum
* BAO
* CMB
* Parameter estimation

---

# Principle 6 — Transparency

Every failed idea, discarded derivation, or unsuccessful implementation should be documented.

Negative results are scientific results.

---

# Principle 7 — Scientific Honesty

Claims made in manuscripts should never exceed what has been demonstrated mathematically or numerically.

Unverified ideas should always be presented as hypotheses or future work.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Develop a mathematically consistent and observationally testable theory whose predictions arise naturally from its physical postulates rather than from phenomenological tuning.
